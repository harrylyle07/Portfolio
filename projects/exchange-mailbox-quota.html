Case study

Fixing mailbox quota bounce-backs

A user's emails were bouncing back because their mailbox had hit its size limit. The underlying cause turned out to be licensing.

Type
Case study
Area
Exchange Online
  • Exchange Online
  • Microsoft 365
  • Licensing
  • PowerShell

Problem

A user's emails were bouncing back with non-delivery reports (NDRs) because their mailbox had hit its size limit.

What I did

  1. Looked into why the mailbox quota was being capped and found a licence mismatch: Exchange Online Plan 1 versus Plan 2.
  2. Corrected the licensing so the mailbox received the right quota.
  3. Set up an in-place archive for the mailbox.
  4. Triggered the Managed Folder Assistant to move older mail into the archive straight away, rather than waiting for its next scheduled run.
# Process the mailbox now so older mail moves to the archive
Start-ManagedFolderAssistant -Identity <user>

Result

With the correct licence in place and older mail moved into the archive, the mailbox was back under its limit and the bounce-backs stopped.
